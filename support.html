Twisty’s Tornado

iPhone and iPad support

Pick a color to start and drag one finger across the scene to move. Switch the level timer before you pick a color. Use the pause button during a run to resume or return to the start screen.

To report a problem, use the Twisty’s Tornado support tracker. Reports are public. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and what happened. Leave out personal information.

Privacy policy for the iOS app

Twisty’s Tornado works offline. The app has no accounts, ads, or analytics. It does not collect or send gameplay data or personal information.

The app stores your sound setting, timer choice, best times, and unlocked worlds on your device. Delete the app to remove its saved data from the device. The iOS app has no account or server that stores these records.

For privacy questions about the app, use the support link above.
